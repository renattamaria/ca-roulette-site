How do I get that file?
  1. In RuneLite, open the Plugin Hub (the wrench icon, then the Plugin Hub button at the bottom), search CA Roulette Export, and install it.
  2. Log in. Wait a few seconds, open your bank once, then type ::caexport in the chat box, or click Export my data in the plugin’s side panel.
  3. It writes %USERPROFILE%\.runelite\ca-roulette\<name>.json. Drop that file above.

Nothing you load here leaves your browser. It’s saved locally so you don’t have to re-import every visit.
